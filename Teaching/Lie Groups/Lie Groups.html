Representation Theory of Finite Groups

This reading group was done with two undergraduates during Spring 2026 as part of the WDRP. The description of the project can be found here. This project was designed with the intent to develop representation theory of the classical Lie groups and thier Lie algebras. Additionally, based on the interests of the undergraduate participants, we studied some applications of SO(3) to both configuration spaces in robotics and to convolutional neural networks and equivariant graph neural networks in machine learning.

The main references we used were:

"Naive Lie Theory" by John Stillwell

Matrix Groups for Undergraduates (Exercises) by Kristopher Tapp

"Lie Group Machine Learning" by Fanzhang Li , Li Zhang and Zhao Zhang

"Modern Robotics: Planning, Mechanics, and Control" by Kevin M. Lynch

Some other useful references we referenced on occassion:

"Lie Groups, Lie Algebras, and Representations" by Brian C. Hall

"Introduction to Lie Algebras" by Karin Erdmann & Mark J. Wildon
